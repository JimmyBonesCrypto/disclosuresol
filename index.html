Official UAP Repositories

The six official government and authorized FOIA archive portals where declassified UFO/UAP files are being released.

01

Primary 2026 Declassification Portal (PURSUE)

Rolling Release

Launched on May 8, 2026, this is the main hub for the "rolling release" of never-before-seen files. It currently features approximately 160 declassified documents and images, including raw infrared footage and original source documents from various agencies.

Access Portal war.gov/ufo/
02

NSA "Top Secret Umbra" FOIA Release

New Appeal

The direct raw repository of the successful May 18, 2026 FOIA appeal victory by the Disclosure Foundation. This database exposes previously redacted text from highly sensitive Top Secret Umbra signals intelligence (SIGINT) logs stretching back to the 1960s.

Access Repository disclosure.org/news/
03

Official UAP Investigative Data (AARO)

.mil

This is the central office for current government investigations. This specific page hosts "cleared for release" imagery and videos, including high-profile cases like the "Gimbal" and "GoFast," as well as more recent technical sensor data.

Access AARO aaro.mil/UAP-Cases/
04

Historical National Archives (NARA)

RG-615

Home to Record Group 615, a consolidated collection of UAP records mandated by the 2024 NDAA. It includes bulk downloads of digitized photos, textual records, and sound recordings transferred from the FAA, NRC, and other federal agencies.

Access NARA archives.gov/…/rg-615
05

FBI Investigative Vault

Reading Room

A digital reading room containing decades of FBI investigations into sighting reports. Recent updates include 16 sections of memos and field reports covering "flying discs" and other anomalies dating back to the late 1940s.

Access FBI Vault vault.fbi.gov/UFO
06

NSA Intelligence Reports

SIGINT / FOIA

This repository consists of formerly classified carbon-paper reports and signals intelligence (SIGINT) documents. These files focus on technical data and historical intelligence gathered regarding unidentified phenomena.

Access NSA FOIA nsa.gov/…/UFOs/
